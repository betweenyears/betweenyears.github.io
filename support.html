Getting started

How does a three-year diary work?

Every day of the year has its own question. You answer it in a few words. A year later, the same date asks the same question, and your answer from last year sits right above it. By the third year, each page holds three answers to one question, so you can see how you've changed.

What is Room to write?

The question is just a start. If a day needs more, Room to write is a separate note for that day, where you can write as much as you like.

I forgot to write last night. Is it too late?

No. Yesterday stays open until 7 am, so you can still fill it in, and your streak holds.

What are the colours and stars?

After you save, you can pick a colour for the day, from cool lavender to warm gold. Each day becomes a star in that colour. Each month gets its own night-sky card in the calendar, and at 100, 500 and 1,000 entries you get a recap of your sky.

How do I change the reminder?

Go to Settings, then the reminders section, to set the time or turn it off. If you've already written that night, you won't get a reminder.

App Lock

How do I lock the diary?

Turn on App Lock in Settings and set a passcode. You can also unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint.

I forgot my App Lock passcode.

On the lock screen, enter a wrong passcode once and "Forgot passcode?" appears. Tap it. Depending on how App Lock was set up, you can get back in with:

  • Your phone's own passcode (the default), or
  • Your recovery code, if you turned phone reset off and saved a code.

If neither is available, the only way back in is to erase the diary on this device. A backup or export lets you restore it afterwards.

Backups and moving phones

How do I back up my diary?

Any phone: go to Settings → Export to save your whole diary to a file. Keep it somewhere safe, because the file isn't encrypted.

iPhone: if iCloud Backup is on, your diary is included in it. You can also save exports to iCloud Drive.

Android: you can also turn on automatic backup to Google Drive. For extra protection, set a backup password: your backup is then encrypted before it leaves your phone. Don't lose that password: without it, nobody can open the backup, including us.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export your diary on the old phone and move the file to the new one (AirDrop, Files, email or a cloud drive). Install Between Years on the new phone, then tap "Restoring a diary?" on the welcome screen, or go to Settings → Import later. On Android with Drive backup, sign in with the same Google account, and enter your backup password if you set one.

Is my diary private?

Yes. It's encrypted and stored on your device. There's no account, no tracking and no server that holds your entries. See the privacy policy for the details.

The beta

How do I join the beta?

Install Apple's free TestFlight app, then open our public beta link on your iPhone and tap Accept.

Will I lose my entries when the app is released?

To be safe, export your diary before switching from the beta to the App Store version, then import it if needed.
