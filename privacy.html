Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

In short: your diary is stored on your device. We don't collect it, can't read it, and don't track you. Your diary only leaves your phone in a backup or an export.

Who we are

Between Years ("the app") is a three-year diary for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what happens to the information you put in it. If you have questions, contact us at betweenyears.support@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your entries: your answers, Room to write notes, and the colour you pick for each day. These are stored in the app's private storage on your device, encrypted (AES). The key is kept separately in your device's Keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android), so a copy of the diary file alone can't be read.
  • Your settings: things like theme, reminder time and app icon, also stored on your device.
  • Your passcode: if you turn on App Lock, only a hash of the passcode (and of any recovery code) is kept, in your device's secure storage. The passcode itself isn't stored.

We don't run a server for the app. There's no account and no sign-up, so we have no copy of your diary.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • We don't sell, share or rent any information.
  • We don't read, scan or use your entries for anything.

Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint

If you unlock the app with biometrics, your operating system does the check. The app only learns whether it succeeded. It never sees or stores your face or fingerprint data.

Reminders

Nightly reminders are scheduled on your device by the operating system. They aren't sent from a server.

Google Drive backup (optional, Android)

On Android you can turn on automatic backup to your own Google Drive. If you do:

  • You sign in with Google, and the app asks only for access to its own hidden app folder in your Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It can't see your other Drive files.
  • You can also set a backup password. With one set, your backup is encrypted on your device before it's uploaded (AES-256-GCM, with the key derived from your password using PBKDF2), and neither we nor Google can read it without that password. If you forget it, the backup can't be recovered, by you or by us.
  • Without a backup password, the backup is stored in your Google Drive unencrypted, protected by your Google account.
  • Your use of Google Drive is covered by Google's own privacy policy. You can remove the app's access and its backup data any time from your Google Drive settings (Settings → Manage apps).

iCloud Backup (iPhone)

Like most apps, Between Years is included in your iPhone's iCloud Backup if you have it turned on. That backup goes to your own iCloud account, is handled by Apple, and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. We don't have access to it.

Export and import

You can export your diary to a file and import it again, for example to move to a new phone. The exported file is not encrypted, so anyone with the file can read it. Keep it somewhere safe. Where it goes (Files, email, a cloud drive) is up to you, and is covered by that service's own terms.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes the diary from your device. To remove a Google Drive backup, remove the app's data from Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps → Between Years → Delete hidden app data). We hold no copy of your diary, so there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Beta testing

If you test the app through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with us, according to Apple's TestFlight terms. These don't include your diary entries.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
