Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

1. The short version

Your diary stays on your device. We do not run servers, we do not have accounts, and we cannot see, read or recover anything you write in Between Years. If you choose to back up, the backup goes to your own iCloud or Google account, not to us.

2. Who we are

Between Years (“the App”) is an independent app based in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the developer of the App, whose details are in section 15. You can contact us at betweenyears.support@gmail.com.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about you. In particular, the App does not include:

  • user accounts or sign-up
  • analytics, tracking or advertising tools
  • crash-reporting services
  • advertising of any kind

The App’s daily questions are built into the App, so it does not need to contact any server to show them.

4. Information stored on your device

Your diary entries, photos, settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. Your diary entries and photos are encrypted with AES-256, using a key that is created on your device the first time you open the App and kept in your device’s secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore), separately from your diary files. Photos you add may contain information such as the date and place they were taken.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to back it up, export it or share it, as described below. We have no access to it.

5. Passcode, biometrics and recovery code

You can protect the App with a passcode. If you turn on Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint unlock, authentication is handled entirely by your device’s operating system. The App only receives a “success” or “failure” result and never has access to your biometric data.

You may choose to create a 12-character recovery code. Your passcode and recovery code are stored securely on your device. Because we never receive them, we cannot reset your passcode or recover your diary for you.

6. Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They do not use a push-notification service, and no information is sent to us.

7. Cloud backup (optional)

You can choose to back up your diary to your own cloud account: iCloud on iPhone, or Google Drive on Android. Backups go directly from your device to your account and never pass through any server operated by us.

Backup password (optional)

  • Backups are not encrypted by the App unless you set a backup password in Settings > Backup password.
  • If you set a backup password, your backup, including photos, is encrypted on your device with AES-256 before it is uploaded. Neither we, Apple nor Google can read it, and it cannot be restored without that password.
  • If you do not set a backup password, your backup, including photos, is uploaded in readable form and is protected only by the security of your Apple or Google account.
  • We never store your backup password. A key derived from it is kept in your device’s secure storage so that automatic backups can run. You can remove the password at any time.

iCloud (iPhone)

  • Backups are stored in the App’s private area of your iCloud account, using Apple’s CloudKit service. We cannot access your private iCloud data.
  • iCloud is provided by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can delete the App’s iCloud data in your iPhone’s iCloud storage settings.

Google Drive (Android)

  • You sign in with your Google account through Google’s own sign-in screen. We never see your Google password.
  • The App requests only the drive.appdata permission. This lets the App create and read its own files in a private, hidden app folder in your Google Drive. The App cannot see, open or change any other files in your Drive.
  • Your Google account name or email address may be stored on your device only to show which account is connected.
  • The backup is stored by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. You can delete it at any time in Google Drive under Settings > Manage apps, and you can remove the App’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The App’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Because cloud backups are designed to be restored through the App, we recommend also using the export feature to keep a copy you can open yourself.

8. Device backups

Your device’s own backup service (such as iCloud Backup on iPhone or Google backup on Android) may include the App’s data. These backups are controlled by Apple or Google and your device settings, not by us. Because the key that protects your diary is kept in your device’s secure storage, it may not be included in a device backup, so a device backup alone may not be enough to restore your diary. Please use the App’s own backup or export feature.

9. Export and sharing

You can export your diary as a JSON file, or as a ZIP file that also contains your photos. Exported files are not encrypted. Anyone who can access the file can read your entries, so please store it carefully.

If you share a screenshot or content from the App using your device’s share options, the destination app’s own privacy policy applies.

10. In-app purchases

If the App offers paid features, purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, payment details or billing address. The App may receive a confirmation from Apple or Google that a purchase is valid, solely to unlock the feature you bought.

11. Children

The App is intended for people aged 13 and over. People aged 13 to 17 should use the App with the permission of a parent or guardian. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws (such as the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, Hong Kong’s Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance and Australia’s Privacy Act) may give you rights to access, correct, export, delete or restrict the use of your personal information.

Because your data is held only on your device and in your own iCloud or Google account, you can exercise these rights directly:

  • Access and export: use the App’s export feature.
  • Correct: edit your entries in the App.
  • Delete: delete entries in the App, delete the App, and remove your cloud backup as described in section 7.

If you have a question about your rights, contact us at betweenyears.support@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

13. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including passcode protection, AES-256 encryption of your diary entries and photos on your device, secure on-device storage for your keys, passcode and recovery code, and optional password encryption for cloud backups. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your device, passcode, recovery code, backup password and Apple or Google account secure.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes, for example if we add new features. We will post the updated policy at https://betweenyears.github.io and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the App before it takes effect.
