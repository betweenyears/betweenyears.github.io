2. Your wellbeing

Some daily questions may bring up memories or feelings that are hard to think about. You never have to answer a question, and you can skip any question at any time.

If you are in distress or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out for support now:

  • In an emergency, call your local emergency number (000 in Australia, 999 in Hong Kong and the UK, 911 in the US and Canada, 112 in the EU).
  • Australia: Lifeline 13 11 14
  • Hong Kong: The Samaritan Befrienders Hong Kong 2389 2222
  • United Kingdom and Ireland: Samaritans 116 123
  • United States: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988)
  • Elsewhere: find a free, confidential helpline in your country at findahelpline.com
