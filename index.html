Your days, as stars

Every entry
lights a star.

After you save, pick a colour for the day, from cool lavender through starlight to warm gold. The day becomes a star and joins the same date from your other years, so every date grows its own small constellation.

Small, on purpose

A few words a night.
That's all it takes.

Room to write

The question is the start. When a day wants more, Room to write gives it a page of its own.

Yesterday stays open

Forgot last night? Yesterday stays open until 7 am, so you can still fill it in and keep your streak.

A gentle nudge

A nightly reminder that says something different each night. Miss a few days and it gets quieter, not louder.

Search your years

Find that one day you wrote about the beach, the job or the friend, across every year at once.

App Lock

Keep it yours with a passcode, Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint. There's a safe way back in if you forget.

Paper or night

A warm paper look by day and a dark night look after, or let it follow your phone. Choose the app icon to match.

Private by design

Your diary stays on your phone.

There’s no account to create. We don’t run a server, so there’s nothing on our side that could read your entries.

Read the privacy policy →

  • Entries are stored on your device, encrypted
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics
  • Optional automatic backup to your own iCloud (iPhone) or Google Drive (Android)
  • Set a backup password to encrypt your backup before it leaves your phone
  • Export your whole diary to a file any time
